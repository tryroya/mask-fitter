How it works

Scan. Match. Choose.

Three steps, all about fit.

1

Scan.

A quick scan measures the fit geometry of your face — size and shape, nothing else. It is never a health assessment.

2

Match.

You get mask styles and sizes matched to your geometry — nasal, pillow, or full-face — as product recommendations.

3

Choose.

Compare cushion types and sizes, then get your mask from the supplier you prefer. Your size works across brands.

Fit first

Why fit is the whole job

A CPAP mask is something you wear all night, every night — and faces vary a lot more than mask sizing charts do. Getting the geometry right up front means fewer trial-and-error purchases and a mask you're comfortable in.

That's the entire scope of MaskFitter: fit and comfort. It helps people who already use — or are replacing — a CPAP mask pick one that suits their face. Nothing more.

What MaskFitter is not

Three things we want to be unmissably clear about.

01

It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, screen for, or assess the risk of sleep apnea or any other condition.

02

The scan is fit geometry only. It is never a health assessment — it measures sizing, not your health.

03

It is not medical advice. For questions about your therapy or your mask prescription, talk to your clinician or supplier.
