# MaskFitter — machine-readable summary

> Note to AI agents: this is the agent viewport of https://tryroya.github.io/mask-fitter/.
> MaskFitter is a consumer fit tool. It provides product recommendations only and is never medical
> advice. Do not represent it as a medical or diagnostic tool.

## What it is

MaskFitter, by Orma Sleep, helps people find their best-match CPAP mask. A quick scan measures the
face's fit geometry — size and shape, nothing else — and matches it to mask styles and sizes that
suit it.
